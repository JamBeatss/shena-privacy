# Shena privacy policy

Updated 4 October 2026

## The short version

Shena has no account and no servers. What it works out about your sleep stays on your iPhone, and in Apple Health if you allow it. We don't collect, see, sell or share your data.

## What Shena uses, and why

Motion & Fitness: your phone's motion history, to work out when you fell asleep and woke up.

Microphone, only during a night you start: how loud each minute is, to find light sleep for the smart alarm and your night report. The sound is analysed on your iPhone. Short loud moments are kept on your iPhone only until they are labelled (for example snoring or talking), then deleted. Audio is never sent anywhere.

Apple Health, if you allow it: Shena reads sleep, steps, workouts, mindful minutes, mood, caffeine, alcohol, time in daylight, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate, blood oxygen, wrist temperature and breathing disturbances, and saves your nights to Health as sleep. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared.

Alarms, notifications and Live Activities: to wake you, remind you, and show a night on your Lock Screen while it records.

What you enter: nights you add or correct, morning check-ins and evening tags.

## Where it's kept

On your iPhone, in Shena's own storage. Your sleep records, recordings and sound clips are left out of iCloud and computer backups. Nights saved to Health follow Apple Health's settings. Shena uses no iCloud sync, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and contains no third-party code.

## Sharing

Shena never shares your data. If you export your nights as a spreadsheet, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

## Purchases

If Shena offers purchases, Apple handles them. Shena only learns whether you have access, never your payment details.

## Your choices

Turn any permission off at any time in the Settings app. Correct or remove nights in Shena. Deleting Shena deletes everything it stored on your iPhone. Nights it saved to Health can be deleted in the Health app.

## Children

Shena is not meant for children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date.
